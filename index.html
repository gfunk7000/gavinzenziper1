ABOUT ME!

Hello! My name is Gavin Zenziper, and I am from the city of Chicago. I'm a freshman here at IU majoring in Cybersecurity and Global Policy. A few things about myself are that I really enjoy the Grateful Dead, and I have been to 36 Dead or Dead-related shows this year alone. Chasing these shows has been incredibly fun because every night, no matter if you're seeing the same band or a different one, the music is always played differently. It is amazing to see how these musicians can speak to each other without using words and create a sense of community and love that is contagious in the best possible way. I also play a lot of golf, and I was the president of my high school's golf club. I enjoy reading books that involve Gonzo journalism, specifically works by Hunter S. Thompson. He is my favorite author, and my favorite wordsmith or lyricist is Robert Hunter. I love writing, and I think naturally letting the pen flow is one of the best forms of expression someone can have. Finally, I hope to improve my reading comprehension and writing skills in this class so I can tackle works that require much more attention and deeper analysis. I would like to be able to take works of literature that I thought were "too hard" and fully read, analyze, understand, and report on what happened in the book.
